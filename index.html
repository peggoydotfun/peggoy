04 · fair by construction

Built so we
can't cheat either.

Sybil-neutral

Balls are linear in stake × time. One wallet or a thousand: same balls, same odds. Farming buys nothing.

Randomness nobody owns

Each Drop uses a drand round fixed by the contract when the week starts, mixed with the block hash and verified on chain.

Three keys, 48 hours

Owner is a 2-of-3 Safe behind a 48 h timelock. It can tune two numbers. It cannot touch stakes, streamed ETH or the pot.

On chain, not in a database

No sign-up list on a server. Every weight is a Staked or Withdrawn event anyone can replay. Winners are addresses, not short codes.

No dust tricks

Starting a reward round needs 0.01 ETH queued or the last round over, so a 1-wei kick can't hold real rewards back a week.

Open source

Contracts, tests and this site are public on GitHub. Re-run any Drop from its seed and get the same winners.
